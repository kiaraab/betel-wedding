# Morais Vineyards — venue facts

Only what we've **seen** (Morais' shared album: 3 photos + 4 videos) or what the venue **publishes**. Anything else is a planning choice, not a venue fact.

## Published by the venue
- Ballroom ~4,000 sq ft; standard capacity 250, max 300 — [Palacio package](https://moraisvineyards.com/weddings-events/the-palacio-package/)
- Hardwood floors, Palladian-style windows, a stage, a dance floor, two outdoor terraces; balcony holds up to 50
- All reception furniture included
- No public floor plan or dimensions → **ask Morais for the floor plan PDF**

## Seen in the album

### Ballroom (photo from the balcony + video IMG_0681)
- Long rectangle, hardwood throughout (the "dance floor" is open floor, not a separate surface)
- Far end wall: 5 arched French doors to the outside
- Both long walls: tall windows; right wall also has one arched French door
- **Round, tiered stage** (3 curved steps) in the near-left corner, next to the balcony end
- **Balcony / mezzanine** overlooking the room from the near end
- Coffered ceiling, gold chandeliers, textured gold-taupe walls
- Furniture shown: 60" round tables, gold chiavari chairs, a wine-barrel cocktail table
- **Near end (ground-level videos IMG_0657, IMG_0658):** the balcony runs across the full width of the near end wall, with a wrought-iron railing. Underneath it at floor level are glass doors with decorative etched sidelights, most likely the main guest entrance. There's also a white single door to the side and a wall clock
- Long walls (ground level): alternating arched French doors and tall arched-top windows, letting in lots of daylight
- A dark low table/cabinet sits under one side window (purpose unknown)

### Ballroom at other couples' weddings (online photos: Simply Breathe Events, Joffoto, Brooke Michelle, Jenna Leigh, Morais' Palacio page)
These show *how the room has been used*, not fixed features:
- Open hardwood dance floor in the middle, tables all around it
- Mixes seen: rounds of 8–10 with gold chiavari chairs; long banquet/family rows with greenery garlands; a long head table near the arched doors
- Under the balcony: double doors; couples sometimes hang a large decor piece (mirror installation) there
- Room proportion looks about 1.6 : 1 (long side runs from the balcony to the 5 arched doors) → working plan **≈ 50 ft wide × 80 ft long** (≈ 4,000 sq ft) until Morais confirms
- Full weddings show 20+ tables, so 60 guests leaves plenty of open/lounge space

### Tasting room (photos + video IMG_0652)
- Tile floor, timber-beamed ceiling, spiral pendant lights
- Stacked-stone fireplace with wine cubbies; wall wine racks
- Carved wood bar in an octagonal sunroom with a skylight; arched windows onto the vineyard
- Black leather lounge seating around round glass tables

### Bridal suite (videos IMG_0761, IMG_0762)
- 4 lit vanity stations with salon chairs (good for MUA + hair planning)
- Arched windows; doors out to a stone terrace with a balustrade

### Garden courtyard (video IMG_0762)
- Grass-grid stone pavers, classical statues on plinths, clipped hedges, Italian cypress
- Likely ceremony / cocktail-hour option — **confirm with Morais**

## Design rule (from Kiara)
The appeal of the reference site (wedding.wawasensei.dev/plan) is **interactivity and animation**. Keep motion meaningful and present: items glide into place, seats fill with a pop, smooth zoom, a 3D view. Minimal copy, but never a static page.
