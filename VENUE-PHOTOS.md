# Morais Vineyards — what 169 Knot-listing photos show

Source: The Knot listing photos, folder `betel-wedding-research/knot/` (NNN = file number; cited below as #NNN). I viewed contact sheets of all 169, then every photo that shows a space or a layout at full size.
Adds to `VENUE.md`; nothing here contradicts the Matterport figures (hall ≈ 50 × 75 ft, stage = columned bay on a long wall).

**Reading the numbers.** "Observed" = visible in the photo. "Est." = my scale from chairs (≈1.5 ft wide), 60" rounds (5 ft), or people (≈2 ft shoulder-to-shoulder); treat as ±25 %, and photos are wide-angle, so distances near the frame edge are stretched. "Uncertain" means I could not tell.
Photos are from many different couples, so they show how the room *has been used*, not fixed rules. Ignore #008 to #012: #008 is an industrial brick warehouse (not Morais); #009 to #012 are a styled shoot with no venue layout.

## 1. Spaces

### 1.1 Ballroom (Palacio hall)

**Fixed features seen**
- Coffered ceiling with **6 gold chandeliers** (#028, #029, #064, #143). Two rows of three along the long axis (#028, #064). The ceiling is high: two storeys (#143).
- **Balcony** at one short end (#029, #064, #143, #061). In #143 (taken from the room, facing the balcony wall) the railed front is a curved bow about 25 ft wide in the middle, with straight railed sections inside arched recesses on each side. It reads as full width, but the bow is only the middle part (uncertain whether the side sections are walkable). Below it, centered on each half of the wall: **two white paneled double doors** (≈5 ft each, ≈15 ft apart, est.), plus a **single door near each corner** (≈3 ft, est.). The middle of the wall between the two double doors is blank. The doors in #143 are solid white, not the etched glass noted in VENUE.md, so the glass entrance may be a second set of doors (uncertain).
- **Stage = domed octagonal bay in a long wall**, framed by two tall white columns with an arch (#031, #058, #061, #100, #135). 3 curved steps faced in painted tile, a polished wood platform, ≈20 ft wide (est.). It sits mostly outside the rectangle; the steps only push a few ft into the hall (#061, #124: the bottom step ends ≈4-5 ft from the arch line, est.).
- **Far short wall:** a row of **5 tall arched French doors/windows with fan transoms**, evenly spaced (#124 shows all 5 clearly; #060, #064, #088 show the same arches). Light, bay-window-height sills; in #061 the doors open onto a balustrade (terrace).
- Long walls: tall arched windows (some with a French-door lower half), a big Palladian window on the stage's wall between the stage and the balcony end (#064), and an arched French door beside it (#124). Wall sconces between windows (#143).
- Hardwood floor throughout; the "dance floor" is simply the open middle (#119, #124, #108).

**Table sizes / shapes / counts seen**
- #028: 12+ rounds visible, white linen, gold chiavari chairs, tall branch-and-flower centerpieces; ≈8-9 chairs each. Long aisles between rows; center left open.
- #060: ≈10 rounds visible down one side, about 9 chairs each (60" vs 72" uncertain).
- #064: head table (pink linen) in the stage bay, ≈10 rounds visible in a loose horseshoe, open floor in the middle. DJ rig and a speaker at the left wall; draped tables (guest book/gifts or vendor) along the right wall (uncertain what they are).
- #143: balcony-end view, 8 or 9 rounds (60", est.) in two rows against the door wall, white linen, gold chairs with chair sashes. Row-to-row ≈10-12 ft and table-to-table ≈9-10 ft center to center in this set-up (est.; this is a tightly packed 150-250 guest layout). Chair backs almost touch.
- #119: rounds of 8-10 along the near edge, a dance-floor circle in the middle, more rounds against the far wall.
- #124: rounds on three sides (left, right, front), open center full of dancers.
- #130, #088, #086, #146: round tables with numbered stands (table number card ≈ 2 ft tall, on top of tall centerpieces).
- #127: a long banquet table (single row of chairs each side, ≈8+ seats per side) running **along a long wall**, black runners, gold chargers.

**Where things were put**
- **Dance floor:** center of the room, tables on 3 sides (#119, #124, #064, #028). First-dance circle in #119: 40 or so standing guests make a ring ≈25 ft across (est.; derived from ≈2 ft per person). Dancing crowd in #124: ≈35-40 people in a ≈25 × 25 ft patch, packed.
- **Head/sweetheart table:** *in the stage bay* in #064 (head table on the platform), #135 (round blue sweetheart table with 2 gold chairs on the platform), #040 (round olive sweetheart table with its cake table beside it, bay window behind). Not seen at the far arched-door wall in any photo, though VENUE.md's online photos show one there.
- **Cake:** on a small round table (≈4 ft, gold linen) on the stage next to the sweetheart table (#135), beside it (#040), by a window (#108 bottom-left), on a wine barrel (#069).
- **Bar:** long black rectangular bar (≈9 ft, est.) in the **corner of the balcony-end wall, beside the right-hand corner door** (#143); a bartender behind it.
- **DJ:** speaker on a stand and a black DJ table at the long wall near the stage (#064, #119, #124). The band/DJ was *not* in the bay in any photo (the bay was the head table).
- **Photographer/video:** along the long walls and on the balcony (#064, #119).
- **Guests flow:** in via the balcony-end doors, rounds filled toward the far (arched-door) end, open floor in the middle, stage/head at the long wall.

**Ceremony inside the ballroom (rain plan, #029, #061, #100, #104, #106, #110)**
- Aisle runs **across the room (perpendicular to the long wall) to the stage bay**; chair blocks sit on both sides of a runner and rows run parallel to the stage wall. In #061/#104 there is also a second block of rows behind/aside, so the room can be filled with 200+ chairs.
- Aisle width: runner ≈4-5 ft (est., ≈3 gold chairs wide in #061); in #029/#104 center aisle looks ≈5-6 ft.
- Chairs: gold or silver chiavari with cushions (#029, #061, #100), white resin chairs in #104. Row pitch ≈2.5-3 ft (est.). First row is about 4-6 ft from the stage steps (#100, #110).
- The wedding party stands **on the floor** at the foot of the steps, either side (bridesmaids left, groomsmen right, #100); couple and officiant stand on the platform.
- Tables for the reception were already in place in #061/#104 at the far side of the hall (draped rounds along the far wall), so the room can hold ceremony + reception together (cocktail hour moves people out, staff flip the chairs).

### 1.2 Pergola ceremony site (outdoor lawn)
- White vinyl-looking pergola on columns with a stone **balustrade** behind it and the pond/fountain beyond (#022, #099, #107, #147, #149, #161). A fountain plume is visible in the pond to the left of the pergola from the guest side (#022, #099, #161).
- **Aisle:** a straight, slightly curving **pea-gravel/exposed-aggregate path** ≈6 ft wide (est.: ≈4 white chairs wide in #099; two ring bearers abreast with a sign fit easily in #081), running from the building side to the pergola.
- **Chairs:** white resin folding chairs, in two blocks either side of the path (#022: each block ≈10 chairs wide and a bit under 10 rows deep from the camera, so ≈150 chairs in total, consistent with the published 150). Row pitch ≈3 ft (est.). Blocks are on grass, the aisle on gravel.
- Pergola ≈35-40 ft wide, depth ≈15 ft with an open stone-paved floor under it (est. from people spacing in #107: 8 people left, 5 right, officiant and couple in the middle).
- **Standing positions (#107, #149):** bridesmaids in ONE line to the left of the couple, groomsmen in ONE line to the right, ≈2.5-3 ft apart, in front of the columns; the officiant stands slightly off-center behind the couple. Flower girls/ring bearers next to the bridesmaids/groomsmen.
- **Recession and flow:** guests sit with the main building *behind* them (#081 shows the building behind the guests); recessional walks back down the same path toward the building (#147, #161).
- Distance from the building to the pergola: looks like ≈100+ ft (uncertain); there is a lawn and a drive between (#013, #022).
- Details seen: hanging paper lanterns (#149), draped chiffon (#099, #107), aisle-end flower stands (#107).

### 1.3 Courtyard (front, with fountain)
- U-shaped building around a paved courtyard with a **round stone fountain with a statue** left of center, a **large tree**, cypress pairs, planted beds and wooden barrels (#056, #101, #001, #023).
- Surface: light poured-concrete paving (not grass-grid pavers like the video clip in VENUE.md). Fountain basin ≈12-14 ft across (est., ≈3× the statue's width).
- **Black wrought-iron bistro tables** (≈2-4 seats, ≈8-9 visible) along the building edges and under the tree (#056, #001); a canopy umbrella on one table (#056).
- The courtyard is where couples **arrive, take portraits, do cocktail hour** and where a white horse-drawn carriage pulls in (#101, #103, #105; carriage on the paved drive too).
- Building faces the courtyard with arched French doors at ground level (those are the tasting-room side) and arched windows with Juliet-style railings on the upper floor (#056, #101).
- No ceremony photo is taken *in* the courtyard.

### 1.4 Terraces / balustrade
- Stone balustrade terrace overlooking the pond, with a fountain jet in the water (#134, #159). Tan aggregate paving. Couples hang drapes on the balusters (#134). This is the "private terrace" for cocktail hour and sunset photos; it is next to the pergola (#099, #022).
- The ballroom's arched French doors open to a balustraded terrace (#061 shows a balustrade through the far-wall doors).
- A **glass-and-iron conservatory** stands apart on a raised brick-stepped base across the lawn from the building (#002, #013, #020). It is empty inside in the photos; I could not tell if it is bookable or just a photo backdrop (uncertain).

### 1.5 Chapel
- Small white chapel (exterior #016, #079, #125, #021; bell-gable roof). Inside: white vaulted room, crucifix, two statue niches, marble floor, a small altar with 2-3 steps (#132, #039, #148).
- Seating: dark wood pews, **2-3 short pews per side visible** (#039, #132); a handful of guests (4 to 6 visible in #132). Seats perhaps 20-30 at most (uncertain).
- It can't take 60 guests. Treat as a small/elopement or photo spot.

### 1.6 Barrel / wine cellar
- Brick barrel-vault cellar, a wall of stacked wine bottles, oak barrels along both sides (#024, #042, #043, #129, #163).
- Passage between barrel rows ≈8-10 ft wide, ≈25 ft deep to the bottle wall (est.). Moody amber light. No seating or tables in any photo: portrait spot only.

### 1.7 Tasting room, bridal suite, bar room
- Only hints: #017, #018 are small interior corners (octagon relief, arched window, display case); no useful layout. Tasting-room, Redondo Room, bar room and bridal-suite layouts are not shown in this set.

### 1.8 Spiral staircase
- Wood-and-iron spiral staircase with a window looking to the patio (#082). Wide enough for a person in a full gown; shown from above only.

### 1.9 Flow between spaces (as photographed)
1. Arrival: drive/carriage into the courtyard (#101, #103, #105).
2. Ceremony: lawn + pergola (guests walk from the building, building behind them), or indoors at the stage bay (rain).
3. Cocktail: courtyard bistro tables and the pond terrace (#056, #134); glow-stick dancing later (#006, #133).
4. Reception: ballroom via the balcony-end doors; the arched far doors open to the terrace.
5. Send-off: glow sticks (#133) inside/at the exit, carriage and courtyard (#101).

## 3. Conflicts with the current site (recommended changes)

Code read: `js/data.js` (`seedRoom`, `seedTables`, `seedSpaces`, `AREA_PRESETS`), `js/layout.js`, `js/spaces.js`, with how the stage is drawn in `js/seating.js` / `js/three-view.js`.

1. **Stage depth is drawn too deep.** `stage: { cx: 0, cy: 41, r: 10, d: 15, steps: 3 }` is drawn as a half-ellipse 15 ft *into* the room (`seating.js` ≈l.182-187; `blockers()` in `layout.js` l.101 reserves x from -13 to **18** ft and y 28 to 54). VENUE.md/Matterport says the 15-ft bay sits outside the rectangle, and the photos show only the 3 steps (≈4-5 ft) in the hall.
   - Change: draw the bay outside the 50 × 75 rectangle and keep only ≈5 ft of steps inside; set the keep-out to about ≈8 ft from the wall (x ≤ 8), y ≈ cy ± 13. That frees ≈10 ft × 26 ft of dining floor next to the stage, and matches tables sitting right up to the steps (#124, #119).
2. **Head table default.** `headTable()` is a 2-seat rect at x 25, y 7 (far wall) and every non-stage style starts there. The Knot photos put the sweetheart/head table in the bay (#064, #135, #040).
   - Change: make "Head on stage/bay" the default (or first button). The `stage` style in `layout.js` (l.243-259) places a 7-ft rect rotated 90°; photos show a round ≈5 ft table with 2 chairs (#135) — use a `round` 2-seat head at about (cx + 6, cy). Add a 4-ft round cake area beside it at about (cx + 6, cy ± 6).
3. **Stale wording.** `layout.js` l.242 ("round stage (near-left corner)"), `TABLE_STYLES.stage` comment, and the "Head on stage" bullet in VENUE.md call the stage "round, near-left corner". The code geometry is already the long-wall bay; update the text (and `owner-ref-6` note) to avoid confusion.
4. **Dance floor.** Seed area is 16 × 18 at x 17, y 13 (center 25, 22), right behind the head table at the far wall. Photos show the dance floor in the middle of the hall with tables on 3 sides.
   - Change: seed 20 × 20 centered at about (25, 37) (halfway down the 75-ft hall; adjust for stage style), and keep 16 × 18 as the minimum (what `suggestTables` falls back to). `suggestOpen`'s 18 × 20 default is fine.
5. **Entrance.** `seedRoom` has a single 8-ft `entrance` centered at x = 25 in the bottom wall; `blockers()` reserves a 14-ft deep clear zone there. #143 shows two ≈5-ft double doors at roughly x 16 and x 31 (centered wall between is blank), plus 3-ft corner doors.
   - Change: replace with two `entrance` openings w = 5 at x ≈ 16 and ≈ 31 (± 3 ft until Morais confirms), plus two `french` 3 ft corner doors at x ≈ 4 and ≈ 46; shrink the clear zone from 14 to ≈8 ft per door (the current 14-ft rule plus the lounge band takes 22 ft off the near end). Keep as "uncertain" in the UI until the floor plan arrives.
6. **No bar / DJ / cake areas seeded.** `seedRoom().areas` has only `dance`. Add: bar 10 × 4 at the near-right corner (x ≈ 38-48, y ≈ 68-72), DJ 8 × 4 on the stage-side long wall (x 0-4, y ≈ 20-28, so it doesn't cover the stage keep-out), cake 5 × 5 in the bay.
7. **Table pitch.** `placeRounds` uses `aisle = 5` → ≈14 ft center to center (60" round: hw 4.5). Real events are tighter (≈10-12 ft, #143). For 60 guests the 14-ft rule is fine, but `packRounds` (gap 3.5) and `placeRounds` (aisle 5) should be a single constant. Suggested 4 ft (≈13 ft pitch) with a floor of 2.5 ft if the room gets tight. Keep `wallGap = 3`.
8. **Ceremony space is the wrong site.** `seedCeremony` is a "Garden courtyard" 40 × 60 ft with hedges, statues and grass-grid pavers, and `suggestCeremony` uses a 5-ft aisle. The photos show the ceremony on the **lawn at the pergola** (gravel aisle ≈6 ft, chairs on grass) or **indoors at the bay**; the courtyard is for arrival, portraits and cocktail (the photos show light concrete, a fountain and a big tree, not pavers).
   - Change: (a) add a **Pergola** ceremony space: lawn plus a ≈6 ft aisle, pergola ≈36 × 15 ft at the end, chairs on grass, no hedges/statues; (b) set `suggestCeremony`'s `aisle` to 6 (matches `seedCeremony`); (c) keep the courtyard as a cocktail/arrival space; (d) offer an **Indoor** option (bay as altar, 6 ft aisle perpendicular to the long wall). The party-dot spacing (2.8 ft, one row per side) already matches #107/#149.
9. **Cocktail space.** `seedCocktail` is the tasting room only (44 × 34, 5 high-tops). Published info and the photos both put cocktail hour in the courtyard/terrace.
   - Change: add a **Courtyard** cocktail layout: fountain (≈13 ft round, left of center), tree, ≈8 bistro tables (2-4 seats) along the building edges; keep the tasting room as the weather option. The tasting room's interior layout is not visible in this photo set, so its numbers stay estimates.
10. **Flow text.** The spaces tabs use "Ceremony → Cocktail → Reception". Photos support that order: pergola lawn, courtyard/terrace, ballroom via the balcony end. No change needed; add the carriage/arrival and a send-off point if the app has a flow view.
11. **Balcony.** `balcony: { depth: 12 }` is unchanged; #143 shows the bow only in the middle ≈25 ft with straight railed recesses on both sides, so it may not be a single 50-ft slab. Uncertain; ask Morais.
